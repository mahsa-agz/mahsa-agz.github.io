Aptitude test practice

Numerical and logical reasoning in the style of SHL, Cubiks and other company tests. The questions are generated, so the numbers are new every time. Today's set is 14 mixed questions that change every day. Do it daily and watch your average time drop.

Pick an answer, then Check answer. Keys: 1–5 to pick, Enter to check, Enter again for next. Use a calculator for numerical, as in the real test.

Cheat sheet

Percent change

(new − old) ÷ old × 100
Divide by the old value, always.

Reverse percent

After +20%: original = new ÷ 1.20
After −20%: original = new ÷ 0.80
Not new × 0.80.

Compound growth

value × (1 + r)ⁿ
Two years of 10% = ×1.21, not ×1.20.
Average yearly growth over n years: (end ÷ start)^(1/n) − 1

Share and ratio

Share = part ÷ total × 100
“A is how many times B” = A ÷ B

Percent vs percentage points

12% → 15% is +3 points, but +25 percent (3 ÷ 12).

Number sequences: check in this order

1. Differences (+). 2. Ratios (×). 3. Differences of differences. 4. Two rules alternating. 5. Two sequences interleaved. 6. Squares, cubes, sum of previous two.

Syllogisms

Draw circles. “All A are B” = A inside B. Only answer True if every possible drawing agrees. If one drawing allows it and another doesn't → Cannot say. Ignore real-world knowledge.

If–then rules

If P then Q.
P → Q is True. Not Q → not P is True.
Q → P? Cannot say. Not P → not Q? Cannot say.

Abstract sequences

Track one feature at a time: rotation, count, fill, position, size. Usually two features change at once.

Pace

Roughly 60–75 s per numerical and 45–60 s per logical question. Skip a question that takes too long; you can't afford to be stuck.
